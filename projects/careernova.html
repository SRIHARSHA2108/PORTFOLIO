Career intelligence platform

CareerNova

A connected career-readiness workspace that turns a user profile into recommendations, skill development, assessments, resume improvement, employment opportunities, and measurable progress.

Live demo ↗GitHub ↗

Overview

CareerNova brings career assessment, skill-gap analysis, personalised learning, resume support, opportunity matching, interview preparation, government-exam practice, and progress analytics into one responsive Django application.

Technology

PythonDjango 4.2Tailwind CSSJavaScriptSQLiteDjango Templates

What I built

  • Session-protected authentication and a personalised career-readiness dashboard
  • Career matching, skill-gap roadmaps, salary signals, and market-demand insights
  • Timed Python and government-exam assessments with automatic submission and answer review
  • Resume analysis, opportunity matching, application tracking, reports, and notifications
  • A contextual career coach and responsive navigation across mobile and desktop

Product experience

The landing experience introduces CareerNova through a visual career-intelligence story before guiding users into the personalised workspace.
